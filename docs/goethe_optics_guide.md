# Goethean Optics Guide

## Dioptric Colours

Goethean boundary colours arise through interaction among light, darkness, and a transparent or turbid medium. The prismatic edge simulator focuses on the phenomena produced as the bright field and shadow boundaries approach and overlap.

## Physiological Colours

Retinal excitation tends toward complementarity. The afterimage exercise presents a saturated stimulus and invites the viewer to observe the eye's subsequent response on a neutral field.
